Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Clave is a local practice tool for tempo, subdivision and accent work. This policy explains exactly what the app does with information. It is written to match what the shipped build actually does, not what it might do in future.

1. What Clave stores

  • Practice material you create yourself: exercise names, tempo values, subdivision and accent patterns, practice routines, and session log entries.
  • App preferences such as your last used tempo, click sound choice and accent level.

All of it is written to the app's own private storage on your device.

2. What Clave never collects

  • No account, sign-up, email address or profile.
  • No microphone, camera, photo library, contacts or location access. Clave only plays sound; it never records it.
  • No analytics SDK, advertising identifier, tracking across apps or websites, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • No third-party AI processing, and no upload of your practice data.

3. Network use

The Clave app makes no network requests of its own. Your practice library works fully in airplane mode.

4. Legal pages

This privacy policy and the terms of use are published as static pages. Opening them uses your device's standard web view, which is the only point at which a network connection may be used, and only to fetch the page you asked for.

5. Data removal

Deleting an exercise, routine or session inside the app removes it from app storage. Deleting Clave from your device removes everything the app stored. There is no server-side copy to request, because none exists.

6. Children

Clave is a general-purpose practice tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, so it collects none from children.

7. Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date above will change.

8. Contact
